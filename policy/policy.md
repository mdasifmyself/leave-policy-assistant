# Northfield Trading Ltd Leave Policy (Sample)

Document HR-POL-001 · version 1.0 · effective 1 July 2026

> Northfield Trading Ltd is a fictitious company. This document is a sample written for demonstration only and is not legal advice.

## 1. Purpose, Scope and Definitions

**1.1** This policy explains the kinds of leave available at Northfield Trading Ltd, how much of each an employee receives, and how leave is requested, approved and recorded. Its aim is that every employee is treated by the same rules and can plan time off with confidence.

**1.2** The policy applies to all permanent, full-time employees of the Company, at every office and warehouse, from their first day of work. Employees in their probation period are covered, with the limits set out in Section 12.

**1.3** Contract staff, part-time staff, interns, trainees and outside consultants are not covered by this policy. Their time off is set by the terms of their own agreement with the Company.

**1.4** The leave year runs from 1 July to 30 June. All yearly leave entitlements in this policy are counted for that period.

**1.5** A working day is any day from Sunday to Thursday that is not a public holiday. A calendar day is any day of the week, including weekly holidays and public holidays.

**1.6** In this policy, line manager means the person an employee reports to directly, Department Head means the head of the employee's department, and Staff Portal means the Company's online system for requesting and recording leave.

## 2. Annual Leave

**2.1** Each employee earns 18 working days of paid Annual Leave in a full leave year, at the rate of 1.5 working days for each completed month of service.

**2.2** A request for 5 or more working days of Annual Leave in a row must be submitted at least 15 calendar days before the first day of the leave.

**2.3** A line manager may approve up to 8 working days of Annual Leave in a row. A longer unbroken period also needs the written approval of the Department Head, and no single period of Annual Leave may be longer than 15 working days in a row.

**2.4** Annual Leave is approved according to the needs of the work. A request is not approved automatically, and the line manager may ask the employee to choose other dates when too many people in the team would be away at the same time.

**2.5** Annual Leave may be taken in full days or half days. A half day counts as 0.5 of a working day.

## 3. Casual Leave

**3.1** Each employee receives 10 working days of paid Casual Leave in a leave year, for urgent or unplanned personal matters.

**3.2** No more than 2 working days of Casual Leave may be taken in a row.

**3.3** The employee must tell the line manager before the working day begins, or as early as possible on that day, and must enter the leave in the Staff Portal within 2 working days of returning to work.

**3.4** Casual Leave may not be joined to Annual Leave, either directly before it or directly after it.

## 4. Sick Leave

**4.1** Each employee receives 14 working days of paid Sick Leave in a leave year.

**4.2** Sick Leave is for the employee's own illness or injury. It may not be used to care for another person.

**4.3** An employee who is too unwell to work must inform the line manager by phone or message before 10 am on the first day of absence, or ask a family member to do so.

**4.4** A medical certificate from a registered doctor is required when Sick Leave lasts 3 or more working days in a row. The certificate must be uploaded to the Staff Portal within 5 working days of returning to work.

**4.5** When all Sick Leave for the leave year has been used, further absence for illness is taken from the employee's Annual Leave balance. When that is also used up, the absence becomes Leave Without Pay.

**4.6** Sick Leave that is not supported by a medical certificate when one is required is treated as Leave Without Pay.

## 5. Maternity Leave

**5.1** A female employee who has completed at least 9 months of service with the Company before the expected date of delivery is entitled to 16 weeks of paid Maternity Leave.

**5.2** At least 8 of the 16 weeks must be taken after the delivery. The remaining weeks may be taken before or after it, as the employee chooses.

**5.3** The employee must inform HR in writing at least 8 weeks before the expected date of delivery and attach a doctor's certificate showing that date.

**5.4** Paid Maternity Leave is given for up to 2 deliveries during employment with the Company. For any later delivery the same period of leave is given without pay.

**5.5** Maternity Leave does not reduce any other leave balance, and Annual Leave continues to be earned during Maternity Leave.

## 6. Paternity Leave

**6.1** A confirmed male employee is entitled to 7 working days of paid Paternity Leave on the birth of his child. The leave must be taken in one block, within 6 weeks of the birth.

**6.2** The request should be entered in the Staff Portal at least 14 calendar days before the expected date of birth. A copy of the birth certificate or hospital discharge paper must be given to HR within 30 calendar days after the leave ends.

**6.3** Paternity Leave is given up to 2 times during employment with the Company.

## 7. Compassionate Leave

**7.1** On the death of a spouse, child, parent, brother or sister, an employee may take up to 5 working days of paid Compassionate Leave.

**7.2** On the death of a grandparent or a parent-in-law, an employee may take up to 3 working days of paid Compassionate Leave.

**7.3** Compassionate Leave must begin within 7 calendar days of the death. The employee informs the line manager as soon as possible and enters the leave in the Staff Portal after returning to work.

## 8. Weekly and Public Holidays

**8.1** The weekly holidays are Friday and Saturday.

**8.2** The Company observes the public holidays announced by the Government. Before each leave year begins, HR publishes the list of public holidays for that year in the Staff Portal. Dates that depend on the sighting of the moon are confirmed by a notice from HR.

**8.3** A weekly holiday or public holiday that falls inside a period of Annual Leave, Casual Leave or Sick Leave is not counted as a day of leave. Maternity Leave and Leave Without Pay are counted in calendar days, so holidays inside them are included.

**8.4** An employee who works on a weekly holiday or public holiday at the written request of the Department Head receives 1 substitute day off. The substitute day must be taken within 30 calendar days of the day worked. After that it lapses and is not paid out.

## 9. Leave Without Pay and Unauthorised Absence

**9.1** An employee who has no paid leave left may apply for Leave Without Pay. It needs the approval of both the Department Head and HR, and may not be more than 30 calendar days in a leave year.

**9.2** No salary is paid for days of Leave Without Pay, and Annual Leave is not earned for any full calendar month spent on Leave Without Pay.

**9.3** Absence from work without approved leave, and without informing the line manager, is unauthorised absence. Each day of unauthorised absence is unpaid.

**9.4** When unauthorised absence continues for more than 5 working days in a row, HR sends the employee a written notice asking for an explanation within 7 calendar days.

**9.5** If the employee does not reply to that notice, or the explanation is not accepted, the Company may take disciplinary action, which can include ending the employment. This is never automatic: it happens only after a fair hearing under the Company's Disciplinary Procedure.

## 10. Applying for Leave and Approval

**10.1** All leave is requested through the Staff Portal. If the Staff Portal cannot be reached, the employee sends the request by email to the line manager with a copy to HR, and enters it in the Staff Portal later.

**10.2** The line manager must approve or decline a leave request within 2 working days of receiving it.

**10.3** If the line manager has not answered within 2 working days, the employee may send the request to the Department Head, who must decide within 2 further working days.

**10.4** A line manager may decline or postpone Annual Leave or Casual Leave for work reasons, and must write the reason in the Staff Portal. Sick Leave supported by the required medical certificate, Maternity Leave and Compassionate Leave may not be declined.

**10.5** Leave is approved only when the Staff Portal shows the request as Approved. Starting planned leave before that is treated as unauthorised absence.

**10.6** An employee may cancel approved leave at any time before it starts. The Company may call an employee back from Annual Leave only in an emergency and with the approval of the Department Head, and the unused days are returned to the employee's balance.

## 11. Carry-Forward and Encashment

**11.1** Up to 6 unused working days of Annual Leave may be carried forward to the next leave year. Carried-forward days must be used by 30 September. Any that remain after that date lapse.

**11.2** Unused Annual Leave above the carry-forward limit lapses on 30 June.

**11.3** Annual Leave cannot be exchanged for money while the employee is still working for the Company.

**11.4** When employment ends, earned Annual Leave that has not been taken is paid with the final settlement at the rate of basic salary, up to a maximum of 18 working days.

**11.5** Casual Leave and Sick Leave cannot be carried forward and cannot be exchanged for money. Any unused balance lapses on 30 June.

## 12. Probation and Notice Period

**12.1** The probation period is the first 6 months of employment. An employee on probation may take Casual Leave and Sick Leave from the date of joining, in proportion to the full months left in the leave year.

**12.2** Annual Leave is earned from the date of joining but may be taken only after the employee has been confirmed in writing.

**12.3** Once either the employee or the Company has given notice to end the employment, Annual Leave and Casual Leave are granted during the notice period only with the written approval of both the Department Head and HR.

**12.4** Sick Leave taken during the notice period must be supported by a medical certificate from the first day of absence.

## 13. Confidentiality of Leave and Medical Records

**13.1** Leave records, leave balances, the reasons given for leave and all medical documents are confidential personal information.

**13.2** An employee may see only their own leave record. A line manager may see the leave dates and leave type of people in their own team in the Staff Portal, but not medical details. Medical certificates are seen only by authorised HR staff.

**13.3** No one may share another employee's leave or medical information with a colleague or with any person who is not authorised to see it. This applies to every channel, including chat tools and automated assistants, which cannot confirm who is asking.

**13.4** A person who needs such information for work must ask HR in writing. HR decides whether the request is allowed and replies through official channels only.

**13.5** A breach of this section is handled under the Company's Disciplinary Procedure.

## 14. Responsibilities, Review and Contact HR

**14.1** Employees are expected to plan leave early and keep their Staff Portal record correct. Line managers answer requests on time and arrange cover for absent team members. HR keeps the leave records, publishes the public holiday list and advises on this policy.

**14.2** HR reviews this policy at least once every 2 years. Changes take effect only after written approval by the Managing Director and notice to all employees.

**14.3** Any question that this policy does not answer should be sent to the HR Help Desk at hr@northfield-trading.example. HR aims to reply within 3 working days.
